Common reasons people call

  • Leaks. Dripping faucets, leaking supply lines, water stains on ceilings or walls, or a meter that moves when nothing is running. For Oklahoma City homes on a slab, describe damp spots, warm floors, or a jump in the water bill.
  • Burst or frozen pipes. Sudden water where it should not be. Shut off the main valve first if you can. During an ice storm, let faucets on outside walls drip and know how to shut off the main.
  • Sewer lines. Gurgling drains, sewage odors, or backups in more than one fixture at once.
  • Water heaters. No hot water, not enough hot water, leaking tanks, or strange noises.
  • Clogs and slow drains. Kitchen sinks, toilets, tubs, and floor drains that back up or drain slowly.

Things to know in Oklahoma City

  • Shifting soil can stress slab foundations and buried lines, so mention any new cracks or damp spots.
  • During ice storms, open cabinet doors under sinks on outside walls and know where the main shutoff is.
  • Repeated backups in more than one drain can point to the main sewer line.

What happens when you call

  1. Your call goes to a call-routing partner that works with independent plumbing companies in the Oklahoma City area.
  2. If a participating professional is available for your ZIP code, you are connected so you can describe the problem.
  3. You and the plumbing company discuss timing, the work, and any costs directly. Zaptu is not part of that agreement.

Availability depends on your ZIP code and the time of day. Zaptu does not perform plumbing work, does not send a named company, and does not book appointments.

Before you call

  • Find your main water shutoff and close it if water is actively leaking.
  • Have your ZIP code ready, plus a short description of what you see and hear.
  • If you smell gas, leave the building and call your gas utility or 911 first.
