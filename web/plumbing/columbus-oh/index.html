Common reasons people call

  • Burst or frozen pipes. Sudden water where it should not be. Shut off the main valve first if you can. In Columbus, let faucets on outside walls drip during a hard freeze and know where the main shutoff is.
  • Water heaters. No hot water, not enough hot water, leaking tanks, or strange noises. No hot water after a cold night can mean the tank, the supply line, or both. Say what you are seeing.
  • Sewer lines. Gurgling drains, sewage odors, or backups in more than one fixture at once.
  • Leaks. Dripping faucets, leaking supply lines, water stains on ceilings or walls, or a meter that moves when nothing is running.
  • Clogs and slow drains. Kitchen sinks, toilets, tubs, and floor drains that back up or drain slowly.

Things to know in Columbus

  • During a hard freeze, open cabinet doors under sinks on outside walls and know how to shut off the main.
  • Clay around central Ohio can shift between wet and dry spells, which can stress buried lines.
  • If the house has a sump pump, check that it runs before heavy spring rain.

What happens when you call

  1. Your call goes to a call-routing partner that works with independent plumbing companies in the Columbus area.
  2. If a participating professional is available for your ZIP code, you are connected so you can describe the problem.
  3. You and the plumbing company discuss timing, the work, and any costs directly. Zaptu is not part of that agreement.

Availability depends on your ZIP code and the time of day. Zaptu does not perform plumbing work, does not send a named company, and does not book appointments.

Before you call

  • Find your main water shutoff and close it if water is actively leaking.
  • Have your ZIP code ready, plus a short description of what you see and hear.
  • If you smell gas, leave the building and call your gas utility or 911 first.
