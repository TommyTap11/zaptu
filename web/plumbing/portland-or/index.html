Common reasons people call

  • Sewer lines. Gurgling drains, sewage odors, or backups in more than one fixture at once. In Portland's rainy months, water coming up through a basement floor drain can point to the sewer line.
  • Clogs and slow drains. Kitchen sinks, toilets, tubs, and floor drains that back up or drain slowly. If a plunger clears it for a day or two and it returns, mention that.
  • Leaks. Dripping faucets, leaking supply lines, water stains on ceilings or walls, or a meter that moves when nothing is running.
  • Water heaters. No hot water, not enough hot water, leaking tanks, or strange noises.
  • Burst or frozen pipes. Sudden water where it should not be. Shut off the main valve first if you can.

Things to know in Portland

  • Water in a basement during the rainy months can come from a drain or sewer line, not only from outside. Describe where it appears.
  • Roots from large street and yard trees can get into older sewer lines and cause repeat backups.
  • Cold snaps and ice storms do happen. Disconnect garden hoses and know where your main shutoff valve is before winter.

What happens when you call

  1. Your call goes to a call-routing partner that works with independent plumbing companies in the Portland area.
  2. If a participating professional is available for your ZIP code, you are connected so you can describe the problem.
  3. You and the plumbing company discuss timing, the work, and any costs directly. Zaptu is not part of that agreement.

Availability depends on your ZIP code and the time of day. Zaptu does not perform plumbing work, does not send a named company, and does not book appointments.

Before you call

  • Find your main water shutoff and close it if water is actively leaking.
  • Have your ZIP code ready, plus a short description of what you see and hear.
  • If you smell gas, leave the building and call your gas utility or 911 first.
