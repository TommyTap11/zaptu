Common reasons people call

  • Water heaters. No hot water, not enough hot water, leaking tanks, or strange noises. In the Las Vegas valley, sediment buildup is a common thread in water heater calls.
  • Clogs and slow drains. Kitchen sinks, toilets, tubs, and floor drains that back up or drain slowly. Mineral scale can narrow faucet aerators and shower heads until flow drops off.
  • Leaks. Dripping faucets, leaking supply lines, water stains on ceilings or walls, or a meter that moves when nothing is running.
  • Sewer lines. Gurgling drains, sewage odors, or backups in more than one fixture at once.
  • Burst pipes. Sudden water where it should not be. Shut off the main valve first if you can.

Things to know in Las Vegas

  • Sediment in a tank water heater can cause popping noises and less hot water. Describe what you hear when you call.
  • Mineral buildup can clog faucet aerators and shower heads and wear out valves sooner.
  • Desert heat is hard on outdoor plumbing and irrigation lines, so mention any wet spots in the yard.

What happens when you call

  3. You and the plumbing company discuss timing, the work, and any costs directly. Zaptu is not part of that agreement.

Availability depends on your ZIP code and the time of day. Zaptu does not perform plumbing work, does not send a named company, and does not book appointments.

Before you call

  • Find your main water shutoff and close it if water is actively leaking.
  • Have your ZIP code ready, plus a short description of what you see and hear.
  • If you smell gas, leave the building and call your gas utility or 911 first.
