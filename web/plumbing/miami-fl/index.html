Common reasons people call

  • Clogs and slow drains. Kitchen sinks, toilets, tubs, and floor drains that back up or drain slowly. In Miami, drains that back up during a downpour are worth calling about even if they clear afterward.
  • Leaks. Dripping faucets, leaking supply lines, water stains on ceilings or walls, or a meter that moves when nothing is running. Condo owners should note whether the leak is inside the unit or coming from a shared wall or ceiling.
  • Water heaters. No hot water, not enough hot water, leaking tanks, or strange noises.
  • Sewer lines. Gurgling drains, sewage odors, or backups in more than one fixture at once.
  • Burst pipes. Sudden water where it should not be. Shut off the main valve first if you can.

Things to know in Miami

  • In a condo or apartment, a leak can reach the unit below. Check whether your building management or association needs to know before work starts.
  • Heavy rain can overwhelm drains. If water is coming up through a floor drain or shower, stop running water until the line is checked.
  • Humid, salty air can wear on exposed fittings and outdoor hose bibs over time, so mention any corrosion you notice.

What happens when you call

  1. Your call goes to a call-routing partner that works with independent plumbing companies in the Miami area.
  2. If a participating professional is available for your ZIP code, you are connected so you can describe the problem.
  3. You and the plumbing company discuss timing, the work, and any costs directly. Zaptu is not part of that agreement.

Availability depends on your ZIP code and the time of day. Zaptu does not perform plumbing work, does not send a named company, and does not book appointments.

Before you call

  • Find your main water shutoff and close it if water is actively leaking.
  • Have your ZIP code ready, plus a short description of what you see and hear.
  • If you smell gas, leave the building and call your gas utility or 911 first.
