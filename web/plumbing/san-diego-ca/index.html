Common reasons people call

  • Sewer lines. Gurgling drains, sewage odors, or backups in more than one fixture at once. In older San Diego neighborhoods, cracked laterals and roots are common reasons for repeat backups.
  • Leaks. Dripping faucets, leaking supply lines, water stains on ceilings or walls, or a meter that moves when nothing is running. Look for corrosion on exposed pipes and hose bibs, especially closer to the coast.
  • Water heaters. No hot water, not enough hot water, leaking tanks, or strange noises.
  • Clogs and slow drains. Kitchen sinks, toilets, tubs, and floor drains that back up or drain slowly.
  • Burst pipes. Sudden water where it should not be. Shut off the main valve first if you can.

Things to know in San Diego

  • Older neighborhoods may have clay or cast-iron sewer laterals that can crack or collect roots over time.
  • Water in much of Southern California is on the hard side, which can lead to scale in water heaters and fixtures.
  • Homes near the coast can see faster wear on outdoor fixtures and exposed pipes from salt air, so point out any corrosion.

What happens when you call

  1. Your call goes to a call-routing partner that works with independent plumbing companies in the San Diego area.
  2. If a participating professional is available for your ZIP code, you are connected so you can describe the problem.
  3. You and the plumbing company discuss timing, the work, and any costs directly. Zaptu is not part of that agreement.

Availability depends on your ZIP code and the time of day. Zaptu does not perform plumbing work, does not send a named company, and does not book appointments.

Before you call

  • Find your main water shutoff and close it if water is actively leaking.
  • Have your ZIP code ready, plus a short description of what you see and hear.
  • If you smell gas, leave the building and call your gas utility or 911 first.
