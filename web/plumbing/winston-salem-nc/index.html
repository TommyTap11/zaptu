Common reasons people call

  • Sewer lines. Gurgling drains, sewage odors, or backups in more than one fixture at once. On a hilly Winston Salem lot, a long sewer lateral can hide a root intrusion or a break. Mention repeat backups.
  • Leaks. Dripping faucets, leaking supply lines, water stains on ceilings or walls, or a meter that moves when nothing is running. Clay soil that shifts between wet and dry spells can stress buried supply lines.
  • Burst or frozen pipes. Sudden water where it should not be. Shut off the main valve first if you can.
  • Clogs and slow drains. Kitchen sinks, toilets, tubs, and floor drains that back up or drain slowly.
  • Water heaters. No hot water, not enough hot water, leaking tanks, or strange noises.

Things to know in Winston Salem

  • Clay that shifts between wet and dry spells can stress pipes under a slab and in the yard.
  • Roots from yard and street trees can get into older sewer lines and cause backups that return.
  • On a cold night, pipes in crawl spaces and along outside walls are more exposed. Know where the main shutoff is.

Before you call

  • Find your main water shutoff and close it if water is actively leaking.
  • Have your ZIP code ready, plus a short description of what you see and hear.
  • If you smell gas, leave the building and call your gas utility or 911 first.
