What Zaptu does

Zaptu is a referral service. It takes a person's contact request for a home service, such as plumbing, house cleaning or pest control, and passes it to independent third-party service providers and lead networks, including Polyares and its service-provider partners.

Zaptu does not perform plumbing, cleaning, pest control or any other home-service work. Zaptu does not employ the providers, does not send a named company, and is not part of any agreement between you and a provider.

A request is not a booking

Sending a request does not book an appointment. No callback, visit, arrival time or price is promised. Whether anyone follows up depends on whether a participating provider covers your ZIP code. You and the provider agree on the work, the timing and any costs directly.

Consent to be contacted

When you send a request and check the consent box, you agree that Zaptu may share your details with Polyares and its service-provider partners, and that Polyares, those partners, or Zaptu may call or text about this request. The details shared are the ones on the request: name, phone, ZIP, service type, optional notes and your consent. Do not submit a request if you do not want a call or text.

Some pages show a request form run by Polyares. A request sent with that form goes straight to Polyares and its service-provider partners, and their own terms and privacy notices also apply to it.

To have a request removed, email hello@zaptu.ai. How Zaptu handles your details is described in the privacy notice.

Your responsibilities

  • Send requests only for yourself, or for someone who has asked you to, with a phone number you are allowed to use.
  • Give accurate details. Requests are for US residential homes.
  • Check that any provider you hire has the license and insurance the work requires, and agree on the work and costs with them before it starts.
  • In an emergency, such as a gas smell or a fire, leave the building and call 911 or your utility first. Zaptu is not an emergency service.
