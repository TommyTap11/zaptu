Privacy

Zaptu collects name, phone, ZIP, service type, optional notes, and consent so a home-service provider or lead buyer can contact you about that request.

We share those details with affiliate or pay-per-call networks and service providers only to fulfill the request. We do not sell unrelated marketing lists.

For plumbing call-to-connect, dialing the tracking number connects you through our affiliate partner; call detail may be shared with that network for billing and quality review.

Form submissions are stored by Netlify and, when the API is hosted, in Zaptu lead logs. Do not submit a request if you do not want a call or text.

Contact the site operator through the GitHub repository linked from the site until a public support email is published.
