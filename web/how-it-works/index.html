How it works

  1. The person asks an assistant, or uses the form.
  2. Zaptu requires name, phone, ZIP, service, and consent.
  3. Cleaning and pest requests are prepared for affiliate routing — we pass them along when a buyer covers the ZIP.
  4. Plumbing uses call-to-connect: we show a tracking number and the person places the call. Assistants must not dial for them.
  5. Other trades are saved until a buyer is live.
  6. A provider may call. Nobody is booked until that company confirms. Zaptu does not perform the work.
