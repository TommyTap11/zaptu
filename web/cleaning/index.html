What this covers

A cleaning request describes a clean at a home: a standard house clean, a deep clean, or a move-in or move-out clean. Zaptu records the request and passes it along when a participating independent provider covers the ZIP code. Zaptu does not do the cleaning, does not send a named company, and does not set a price or a date.

  • House cleaning. A standard clean of kitchens, bathrooms, floors and living areas. Say how many bedrooms and bathrooms the home has.
  • Deep clean. A more detailed clean, such as inside appliances, baseboards, cabinets and built-up grime. Say which rooms or areas come first.
  • Move-in or move-out cleaning. A clean of an empty or nearly empty home before or after a move. Say whether the home will be empty and the move date, if known.
  • Apartments, condos and houses. Say the home type, the floor and whether there is an elevator, and any building rules about access or parking.

What to include in your request

Add the details below in the notes field. A participating provider can use them to understand the request. They do not set an appointment, and Zaptu does not perform the work.

  • The type of clean: house cleaning, deep clean, or move-in or move-out.
  • The number of bedrooms and bathrooms, or the rough square footage.
  • The home type: apartment, condo, townhouse, or house.
  • Pets, allergies, or products to avoid.
  • A rough timeframe, such as a move date. A request does not set an appointment.

What happens after you send a request

  1. Zaptu records your request and passes it along when a participating independent provider covers your ZIP code.
  2. If a provider takes the request, they contact you by phone or text to talk through the problem.
  3. You and the provider agree on timing, the work, and any costs directly. Zaptu is not part of that agreement.

A request is not a booking, and a callback is not promised. Zaptu does not perform the work and does not send a named company.

Before someone follows up

  • Put away valuables, medicines, and anything fragile before anyone comes in.
  • Mention pets in the home and whether they will be there.
  • Note any surfaces or products that should not be used, such as natural stone or a household allergy.

Common questions

Does Zaptu do the cleaning?
No. Zaptu passes requests to participating independent providers and does not do the cleaning, send a named company, or set prices.
Is a callback or arrival time promised?
No. A callback depends on whether a participating provider covers the ZIP code on the request, and any timing is agreed directly with the provider.
Who checks licensing and insurance?
The homeowner does. License and insurance rules vary by state and by the job. Zaptu does not check them and does not do the cleaning.
What detail helps with a cleaning request?
The type of clean, the number of bedrooms and bathrooms or the square footage, the home type, pets or allergies, and a rough timeframe such as a move date.
